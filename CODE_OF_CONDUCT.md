# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

This project welcomes contributors of every background. We want participation to
be a harassment-free experience for everyone, and we ask everyone involved to
help keep it that way.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give and accept technical criticism gracefully; critique ideas, not people.
- Keep discussion focused on the contract, the audit, and their use cases.
- Respect differing viewpoints and prior decisions, and explain your reasoning.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, and any other
project communication — and when an individual is representing the project in
public spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately by email to
[lim@limsumin.com](mailto:lim@limsumin.com). Do not open a public issue for a
conduct report, and do not include details about an incident in any public issue
or pull request.

Conduct reports are separate from security reports. A security vulnerability
should follow the private process in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md), not this
channel.

Reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly and discreetly as the maintainers
are able.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards above and may take
action they consider appropriate, including editing or removing contributions,
and warning or blocking a participant for behavior that violates this code.
Enforcement aims to be proportionate to the conduct and consistent across
participants.
